An entertainment partnership

A partnership that starts
with the story.

Qantica combines QIP, its proprietary script curation method, with a Showcase connecting projects and audiences. The opportunity for Avalanche begins while original work and its community are still taking shape.

01 / DEVELOP

Original
creative work.

QIP analyzes scripts and guides revision. Authors develop the story and make the creative decisions.

02 / DOCUMENT

A traceable
project history.

Document how the work evolves, creating a foundation for later verification and review.

03 / CONNECT

An emerging
audience.

Showcase introduces projects and creates opportunities to measure interest and repeat participation.

04 / EXTEND

Future funding
and services.

Connect suitable projects to payments, financing and licensing opportunities with ecosystem partners.

Human-authored.
Non-generative.

QIP supports analysis and revision without generating or rewriting the creative work. Authors retain creative decision-making.

Qantica does not use submitted scripts to train AI models.

Why this matters to Avalanche: a repeatable way to bring developing IP, project records and audience relationships into its entertainment ecosystem. Opportunities to explore: verification, member access, creator payments and future project financing.
